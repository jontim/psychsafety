Color Usage Guidelines

Primary Blue (#005575)

This deep blue conveys stability, trustworthiness, and professionalism. Use it for:

  • Header backgrounds
  • Primary navigation
  • Section headings
  • Footer backgrounds

Bondi Blue (#0095C8)

This vibrant blue adds energy while maintaining professionalism. Use it for:

  • Hero section backgrounds
  • Secondary buttons
  • Icons and graphic elements
  • Highlighted information

Pop Orange (#F37322)

This warm orange creates energy and encourages action. Use it sparingly for:

  • Call-to-action buttons
  • Accent elements
  • Highlighting important information
  • Small decorative elements

Connect Yellow (#FDBB13)

This bright yellow conveys optimism and clarity. Use it selectively for:

  • Secondary accents
  • Highlights in infographics
  • Background for notifications
  • Icons that need to stand out
