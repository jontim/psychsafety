# Voices for The Shadow Fell

One Octave voice per speaking character, saved in your Hume voice library under exactly these names. The world pack already references them; until a voice exists under its name, Octave designs one on the fly from the description, so nothing breaks while you work through the list.

Two ways to make them:

1. **Hume platform, by hand.** Voices, design a voice, paste the description and the sample line, pick the candidate you like, save it under the name below.
2. **The script.** `npx tsx scripts/design-voices.ts --audition` renders three candidates per character into `art/voices/` for you to listen to, then `npx tsx scripts/design-voices.ts --save soraya=2` saves candidate 2 as the named voice. `--list` shows what your library already holds.

Accent canon comes from the vault: Halyran royals and court speak with a refined, regal British Empire accent; Halyran commoners with a warm North African French-accented, Arabic-inflected, melodic lilt. Thorbin's written lines carry a Scots lilt ("Goin' somewhere are ye, laddie?").
