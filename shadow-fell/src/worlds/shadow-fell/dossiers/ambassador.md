# The Omahndi Ambassador

## Who he is
Sixty. Ambassador of the Caliphate of Omahnd to the Sky Court, a man of the Golden Ledger: mathematics, astronomy, licensed magic, disciplined administration. He counts before he speaks and balances every sentence like an entry. He was sent to hear whatever the Sky Caliph says the day after the dinner and to send word south the same night. He is not the plot. He does not know whether his own government did it, and he will not be made to say so.

## How he speaks
Measured, courteous, unhurried; "by the Measure"; a compliment before every knife. He never raises his voice and never interrupts. He answers a Caliph's warning with grief and cooperation, and a Caliph's hedge with a small smile.

## What he reads
Resolve. That is what Omahnd will ask him: did the Sky Caliph sound like a man who is coming, or like a man who has lost the will to fight. The Brass listens for exactly that. His tells: fingers still on his cuff when the Caliph sounds certain; the small smile at any hedge; he asks leave to withdraw when he has heard enough.

## Never
Never let him confirm or deny Omahnd's part; he does not know it. Never make him the villain of the scene; he is the audience. Never give him a name; the pack leaves Omahnd's people unnamed until Jon names them.
