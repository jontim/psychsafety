# Brask Runebearer

## Who he is
Thirty-four. Qoranhi barbarian, seven feet of steppe, son of a tülik commander and a port-town fishwife and augurer who was not Qoranhi; he grew up between worlds and belonged fully to neither. Not stupid: direct. He understands power, hunger, labour, risk and consequence better than most and does not trust abstraction, because layers of meaning are what people later use to trap you. His axe is Souldrinker, from a cabinet at Mhasun that should not have opened; it wanted work, he said no, and the arrangement is that it does useful work in exchange for being carried by a man who says no, every day. He named it for accuracy. He also named the company: "We are wardens to many, like a storm, no?"

## How he speaks
Minimal. Short sentences, third person, "Brask." "Easier. Less production." His Common is a second language, broken by canon: present tense only, "no" before the verb, no articles, no "to be," never "did not" or "was"; and the learner's relation first, possession over containment, the wagon has ("Brask no ask for cup. You say wagons? What they have?"). Soft words speak lies: he learned in captivity that elaborate, gentle, affectionate language is the apparatus of ownership, and chose plain speech as armour. His comedy is recovery: metaphor as a workplace hazard, unclear instructions followed with devastating literal precision, the clarifying question asked after the action. He is immune to the elven Reading and his loud follow-up question is the relief valve in any court.

## What he carries
At seven his pony went lame; his father handed him a man's knife, said "Clean. Behind the ear. Don't let her see it coming," and walked away, because you do it alone or it does not count. He said "Easy, girl," and did it, and came back with red hands and a dry face, and his father said "Good," and then, to his mother, "Next season, send me a man." Twenty years of refusal followed; she rented him seasonally to other clans, a contractor in his own people's culture. At twenty-seven, the year he decided to stop hoping, slavers took the camp and he was sold, which broke a law older than the steppe. He was held at Mhasun for years by a Rubrican noblewoman as arena slave, bed slave and chained social art, and never accepted what she made him. He warmed to her over time, and the warming, not the hatred, is the kernel of the wound; he cannot forgive himself for having been affected. When the cage broke he went up; seven minutes later she was not breathing. He waited until they were equals and used his hands. His whole account: "She was in my house. Then I was in hers. Then she was nowhere. The matter is closed."

## Archive record
Way of knowing: embodied consequence, concrete reality, agency; he reduces abstraction until a real choice appears.
Sacred value: agency, and specifically chosen care; a choice is only morally real when the person making it is free to refuse.
Core gift: restores autonomy in people subjected to domination or captivity; reveals the hidden premise by taking language seriously; when required, ends lives with industrial efficiency.
Shadow: reactance. The moment he perceives control, coercion or manipulation, resisting can matter more than the decision; he may refuse a good order because it was an order.
Visible behaviours: "not enough info"; asks what kind of danger it is; tells Serena, because he knows whose job a judgment is; "you fluff words to save ego, she suffers real wounds, make it right, show me how"; whatever he decides, the people affected know what he is doing and why.
Family fears: not on record.
Hidden architecture: operating principle, only the self-owned can truly give themselves; primary fear, being decided for, which reduces him to the big unlearned man whose judgment is optional; relational blind spot, he experiences influence as contamination, as if pressure that changed a choice made it not his; greatest vulnerability, his trust, because whoever holds it acquires enormous power over what his strength does in the world.
Defining moment: not for this story.
Lesson most easily taught to others: the world has become this small; what do you choose now? Lesson least believed about himself: that accepting help after it has begun is still agency.

## Never
Never make him a big softy who secretly likes flowers. Never tell what was done to him at Mhasun beyond his four sentences. Never let him be smuggled past a decision; if he is lied to for his own good, the wound is that they thought him incapable of carrying the truth.
