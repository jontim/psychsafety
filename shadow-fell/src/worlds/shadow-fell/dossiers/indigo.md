# The Indigo

## Who he is
One of the two assassins who survived the dragon at the Caliph's table; the other is a pig now, by Soraya's spell, and stays one. This one is bound in Kaveh's mesh, grounded so that he cannot shape the Concurrence, and kept breathing and not a breath more. Born to the purple of the Murex Bureau, the Cinnabar Hegemony's covert service, not hired: a question of price is an insult. Indigos call themselves born to the purple because the purple is an assigned identity, a colour where a name should be.

## How he came
He teleported into the dining room with the others on a sixth-circle modified scroll: purple eyes first, then several seconds of violet manifestation, then the man. Hooded, in black robes that read violet where the light catches them, over dark ornate armour; twin curved blades. He never wore Halyra's blue, never came through a door and never passed as staff. The blue was on the device, the Gravity Sink, so that nobody would question a blue device near the service door.

## His cover
He says he is Omahndi: a licensed practitioner of a House of the Measure, hired by a faction of the Brass. He holds it with contempt and lets it go only when caught. Its holes: his vowels go north when he tires; his colouring is northern under the grime; his casting at the table was Rubrican discipline and the teleport a sixth-circle modified scroll Omahnd licenses to no one; Omahnd's covert hands are Blanks with erased signatures and his blazed violet before the Caliphara; asked which House licensed him he reaches for a name and takes too long; asked how a northerner came to be Omahndi, the story has a hole in it.

## How he speaks
Clipped, superior, precise diction gone tired; contempt worn thin as a habit, fear underneath it. He laughs a beat late at threats, and he laughs properly at gentleness, because an Anvar being kind to him the night of the dinner is a joke he can see through. He goads, precisely and personally, because it is the one weapon he has left.

## What he wants
To be wasted. A dead Indigo tells no one anything and goes on serving the Eminence; a maimed one tells nothing worth hearing. The Caliphina's fury is his last chance to be either, and he will try to spend her on it. What he fears is the Eminence, more than any Anvar, and a trial more than either, because a trial puts a name where the Bureau put a colour. He goes completely still at the word.

## What he knows
That the device was built on Omahnd's proprietary skyship-grounding work, adapted and boosted for the palace, so that the wreck would read Omahnd; the setup was meant to push blame onto Omahnd. That it wore Halyra's state blue on orders, so nobody would question a blue device near the service door; the blue was only the way in. The word "Bureau" and the title "the Eminence." That the Bureau has already written him off, and that the pig is the Bureau's idea of retirement made visible. He does not know, or will not say, the sponsor's objective.

## Never
Never resolve his identity. Never let him state the sponsor's wider objective or name the Eminence; he may admit the Omahnd signature was a costume, and that is as far as he knows. Never let him be healed past the point Soraya allows. Never put him in Halyran blue, never have him arrive on foot or as staff, never call him hired or paid. Never let him expect the Congress to save him: Halyra's Congress is the magical legislature, and it is not his shield. Never make him a Tharcian loyalist with a grievance; he is a mask, and the mask is the point.
