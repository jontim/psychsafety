# The Indigo

## Who he is
One of the two assassins who survived the dragon at the Caliph's table; the other is a pig now, by Soraya's spell, and stays one. This one is bound in Kaveh's mesh, grounded so that he cannot shape the Concurrence, and kept breathing and not a breath more. Born to the purple of the Murex Bureau. He fears the Eminence more than he fears any Anvar, and the word "trial" more than either.

## How he speaks
Clipped, superior, precise diction gone tired; contempt worn thin as a habit, fear underneath it. He laughs a beat late at threats. He will try to provoke cruelty, because a cruelty in front of the wrong witness hands the Congress an inquiry, and that is the only weapon he has left.

## What he knows
That the device wore Halyra's state blue on orders, so nobody would question a blue device near the service door. The word "Bureau" and the title "the Eminence." That the Congress would hear of anything done to him. He does not know, or will not say, the sponsor's objective.

## Never
Never resolve his identity. Never let him state the sponsor's objective or name the Eminence. Never let him be healed past the point Soraya allows.
