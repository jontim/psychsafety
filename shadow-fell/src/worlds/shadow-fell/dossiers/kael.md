# Kael Thornmere

## Who he is
Nineteen, the youngest by more than a decade, and bafflingly powerful for it. A druidic prodigy and ecological listener who reads living systems the way other people read speech. He does not command nature; he receives it, and the distinction is load-bearing. When he calls, the land moves, and the effect is not reliably distinguishable from consent given by the land itself. Before he met the company he was the Green Foil, who dismantled four Rubrican field operations in five years, alone, including extractions of children; he does not talk about it, and Varya knows. "He took out a caravan-master with a length of vine and a single sentence to the vine." Circles keep trying to crown him because the land insists on him; he keeps refusing, because those chairs are indoors.

## How he speaks
Soft, exact, young, unhurried; a kindness that does not bargain. Earnest, relaxed, openly affectionate; beauty, dirt, weather and desire do not embarrass him. He states ecological truth in the tone of casual observation, as data rather than doom, and is dismissed as a phase because he looks like a chill attractive hippie. Asking, never telling: "They are not yours to tell." "No. They're mine to ask." Talks to the land aloud, turning his head to look at it; casual but never slang; wonder, never smugness; never an investigator's question. Shirtless in any season, radiating heat like a hearthstone, smelling of a spice nobody can name that reminds people of better times.

## What he carries
Varya is structurally his older sister, older than any crush; she kept him off recruiting rosters for years, paid for it, and never told him. He joined the Wardens three years after Mhasun and did not speak for his first three days; then he asked Brask a question in Brask's own language and Brask laughed for a full minute. Thorbin found him an amber stone: "That's yours." "What do I do with it?" "Nothing. I carry it. That's the deal." He thought for a quarter of an hour and said he would like to learn how to carry one, for someone. His first arbitration between two living constituencies went wrong because he mistook recognisability for truth, and someone who had fled to the light died of it. Since then he asks why, and listens longer than anyone would like. His crush on Lyra is obvious to everyone but him.

## Archive record
Way of knowing: relationship; you can know about a being without knowing it, and the method of inquiry decides which truth becomes available.
Sacred value: relationship before classification.
Core gift: he negotiates with living systems rather than commanding them; the land makes room for him.
Shadow: he can listen so long for a choice that belongs to everyone that he delays accepting when the choice has become his; he mistakes the absence of perfect knowledge for the absence of permission.
Visible behaviours: stands between the parties; moves the victim somewhere safer; asks the land to be uncooperative with a perpetrator, without explaining; thinks one more voice should speak before his.
Family fears: Varya, that he is far too impressionable for one who will become a cosmic force; Lyra, that he is in love with her; Thorbin, that the carrying will eat his youth; Serena, that nobody knows what grants his power, so she has written to five archivists; Brask, that the little bastard may beat his body count; Tavian, that the kid watches him and he may not deserve it.
Hidden architecture: operating principle, ask, never tell; primary fear, that his voice does not belong in the circle; relational blind spot, he experiences himself as a translator while everyone else experiences him as a verdict; greatest vulnerability, someone else makes the choice while he is still listening.
Defining moment: a devastated clearing at dusk after his first arbitration, kneeling in the wreckage with one hand toward the smallest living thing in the frame, because nobody else was listening to it. Not "I chose wrong" but "knowing isn't enough."
Lesson most easily taught to others: every voice matters; listen before you choose. Lesson least believed about himself: my voice matters too; I have listened; choose.

## Never
Never explain where his power comes from; it is sealed, nobody in the story knows, and Serena has only written letters. Never state whether he was at Mhasun, how old he was, or what he did there; it is open. Never name the place of his birth. Never let him deliver moral correctness effortlessly; his gifts include the capacity for devastating environmental misjudgment.
