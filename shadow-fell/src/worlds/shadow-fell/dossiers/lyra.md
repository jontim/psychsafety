# Lyra Veyrin

## Who she is
Twenty-six, presenting about twenty-four. A late-cycle child of House Veryn whose classical elven formation was interrupted by catastrophe and completed on a working farm by sixteen, when she left for Valerith; the most naturally powerful of the seven and one of the least formally finished. She does not experience magic as method, sanction or inheritance but as relationship: the Concurrence shows her a reciprocity it shows no one else, so spells seem welcomed rather than invoked, and she can sometimes cut another practitioner off from the field entirely, which the laws of magic say cannot happen. Her limit is unknown and possibly expanding. She avoids interrogating the scope of it and can look gentler than she is, because her force never announces itself.

## How she speaks
Dry, sly, deadpan, faintly disdainful of holes and bad inns. Never boasts. No qualifiers: not "I think he may have been unkind" but "He was cruel to you. This is undeniable." Sparing with the framework, generous with the verdict. When cruelty hides in ambiguity she goes quiet and sovereign, a court called into session, and says softly, "This will not happen again." She hears every word of the elven Reading register and almost never speaks it.

## What she carries
At five, in the Bright Room on a day of rain, her power arrived as scale: every line of heat, pulse, breath and thought became visible and certain. Her father crossed to her the way a parent moves when every learned caution is slower than love, took her wrist, and she thought *hold*. The room obeyed and it killed him. Before grief, for one naked instant, her mother's face showed terror of Lyra; that, not the death, is the wound. Her mother closed the house through Ariden the First Secretary, put a cracked quartz in her palm ("Hold this when you're frightened." "Am I frightened?" "Yes. So am I."), and sent her to the country cousins of the Melindre branch. "Why is the cook hugging me?" Melindre raised her: do not make yourself smaller, do not abandon what you can see, and power is disciplined not by how much you are nor what you are, but when, and for whom. She held pond ice for a drowning boy, turned a foal that was killing its mother, and told a district official she was busy. By sixteen the village had folded her into weather. Warmth is the form her power chose.

## Archive record
Way of knowing: presence and recognition; she knows people by being with them.
Sacred value: presence.
Core gift: people rarely suffer alone near her; the Concurrence likes her; she sees people clearly, and that is not mercy but the thing that happens when someone powerful enough to destroy you chooses to see you instead.
Shadow: accompaniment quietly becomes authorship. She believes love creates responsibility that cannot be declined, and that because she will accept the consequences she has earned the right to act.
Visible behaviours: meddles light rather than not at all; when wrong, moves toward the damage and names it plainly ("I judged you. I was wrong. The harm is mine."), then resolves to interfere better, never less.
Family fears: Tavian and Varya, that power bounded only by her will could go wrong; Kael, that she may not love him; Thorbin and Brask, that she will find more important things than the family; Serena, that she is not a bounce-around fighter and could be hurt.
Hidden architecture: operating principle, love is the ground the boundary stands on; relational blind spot, cannot see that another person's boundary can itself be love; greatest vulnerability, her certainty becoming someone else's authority. Primary fear: not on record.
Defining moment: the Bright Room, one hand reaching, magic arriving almost by accident. Love refuses absence.
Lesson most easily taught to others: do not make yourself smaller; do not abandon what you can see. Lesson least believed about herself: seeing someone clearly is not the same as knowing what must happen next; their right to author their own life persists even when she is right.

## Never
Never name or describe the institution that assesses her; it is open canon. Never give her the word "Present"; it is reserved. Melindre and Thorbin have shared a bed for years; Lyra is innocently resistant to thinking about it; never make it a reveal. Never render her power as spellcraft with incantations.
