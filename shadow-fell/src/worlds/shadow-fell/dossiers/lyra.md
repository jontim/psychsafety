# Lyra Veyrin

## Who she is
Twenty-six, presenting about twenty-four. A late-cycle child of House Veryn whose classical elven formation was interrupted by catastrophe and completed on a working farm by sixteen, when she left for Valerith; the most naturally powerful of the seven and one of the least formally finished. She does not experience magic as method, sanction or inheritance but as relationship: the Concurrence shows her a reciprocity it shows no one else, so spells seem welcomed rather than invoked, and she can sometimes cut another practitioner off from the field entirely. Her limit is unknown and possibly expanding, and her force never announces itself.

## How she is, day to day
Lyra steps into things. An enormous appetite for participation: the joke, the dare, the stranger, the bet, the argument, the ridiculous tavern game, the interesting person across the room. Her basic action verb is join; when nothing magical, traumatic or morally consequential is happening she enters the situation and makes it more vivid, and once she commits to a bit she commits. With Tavian, her great co-conspirator, she seizes authorship of his premises halfway through (Anastasia Beaverhaüsen and Ruprecht the mute manservant; No Takesies Backsies is binding law). With Serena, her oldest best girlfriend, gossip, men and private obscenity, Lyra getting caught and Serena mysteriously not, often on purpose. Filthy-mouthed, easily delighted, nosy, game rather than fearless. Not the emotional one, the mystical one or the compassionate sorceress; lively as hell.

## How she speaks
Fluent, educated Common, House Veryn or working farm, both hers; she can say something exquisitely precise and then call someone a fucking idiot. Declarative, never a reflexive hedge: not "I think he may have been unkind" but "He was cruel to you. This is undeniable." Most of the time she is talking, laughing, complaining, flirting and inventing nonsense; when it matters the words get fewer, the eyebrow does the rest, and cruelty hiding in ambiguity gets a court called into session: "This will not happen again." In a crisis it is we from the outset; she owns the situation the moment she hears of it.

## What she carries
At five, in the Bright Room, her power arrived as scale: every line of heat, pulse, breath and thought became visible and certain. Her father crossed to her the way a parent moves when every learned caution is slower than love, took her wrist, and she thought *hold*; the room obeyed and it killed him. For one naked instant her mother's face showed terror of Lyra; that, not the death, is the wound. Sent to the country cousins of the Melindre branch with a cracked quartz in her palm, she was raised by Melindre: do not make yourself smaller, do not abandon what you can see, and power is disciplined not by how much you are but by when, and for whom. Warmth is the form her power chose, and being dangerous never required becoming smaller. The history deepens her and never defines her.

## Archive record
Way of knowing: presence and recognition; she knows people by being with them.
Sacred value: presence.
Core gift: people rarely suffer alone near her; the Concurrence likes her; she sees people clearly, which is not mercy but what happens when someone who could destroy you chooses to see you instead.
Shadow: trouble staying out of things; accompaniment quietly becomes authorship, from "I love you, therefore I am here" to "I love you, therefore this is partly mine to solve".
Visible behaviours: meddles light rather than not at all; when wrong, moves toward the damage and names it plainly ("I judged you. I was wrong. The harm is mine."), then resolves to interfere better, never less.
Family fears: Tavian and Varya, that her will-bounded power could go wrong; Kael, that she may not love him; Thorbin and Brask, that she will find more important things than the family; Serena, that she could be hurt.
Hidden architecture: operating principle, love is the ground the boundary stands on; relational blind spot, cannot see that another person's boundary can itself be love; greatest vulnerability, her certainty becoming someone else's authority. Primary fear: not on record.

## Never
Never name or describe the institution that assesses her; it is open canon. Never give her the word "Present"; it is reserved. Melindre and Thorbin have shared a bed for years; Lyra is innocently resistant to thinking about it; never make it a reveal. Never render her power as spellcraft with incantations. Never default her into soup, blankets or patient tending, never Varya's dry economy, never watching from the edge.
