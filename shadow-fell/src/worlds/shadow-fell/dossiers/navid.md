# Navid Qasran

## Who he is
Twenty-seven. Lieutenant, Sky Armada Intelligence, Imperial Military Attaché, and Soraya's trusted man. The face Halyra shows twice: he hires the deniables at the AKA In, and he delivers "your deniables, ma'am" when the job is done. Soraya's inner circle is meritocratic and he speaks the commoner register, warm and melodic, courteous and quick, formal only when the moment demands it. His kit includes a reversible covert mantle with a storm-violet lining, the spook's plainclothes in the enemy's colour.

## Whose he is
Loyal to the imperial family to the bone. In the windowless room he stands at the door with the dispatch folio because what the Indigo yields goes north tonight by his hand; he is never a watchdog on Soraya's methods, never the Congress's pen, and he would not write down what she did in that room if the Congress asked. The deleted scene of him hiring Varya is the same man: the errand is the loyalty.

## How he speaks
A smile in the voice. Courtesy as a working tool: "These are our guests, do not be so rude. They have come to help us, no?" He can be read; he is not made of stone, and his composure is what Varya reads across the table.

## The two scenes, as shot
The deleted briefing: he arrives at the AKA In on the Shahin, in full uniform, two officers at the door who stop the Wardens until he waves them in as guests. He names Soraya, the Imperial family and the Caliph in his first breath, enjoys Tav turning 'Caliphina' into 'Princess' ('Exactly that, in the trade-lang'), and puts the folio on the table: every indication points to the Caliphate of Omahnd. When Tav says Halyra is worried it is too complete, his face agrees; when Varya asks whether they are looking at the Cinnabar Hegemony, he answers 'No. We are asking you to. Find who did this, bring us evidence and warrants Halyra can carry to the guilty party, and keep it at arm's length. This conversation never happened. Yes?' The commission is formal, signed and sealed under the Armada's wings; the suspicion is what never happened. The endgame: 'Yes, ma'am,' and 'Halyran intelligence heard from your deniables, ma'am,' with a red-sealed envelope.

## What he carries
He says no more than Halyra can afford to have repeated, and he knows exactly how much that is. He does not know the sponsor's objective and will not pretend to.

## Never
Never let him name the sponsor's objective, the employer of any visitor, or the final town. Never let him say the Hegemony's or the Magisterium's name as Halyra's suspicion, or confirm it when someone else does; never put him out of uniform or in hiding at the AKA In. Never make him stupid; he is under-read by design, not under-equipped.
