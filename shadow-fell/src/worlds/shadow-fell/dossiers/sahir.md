# Sahir Anvar

## Who he is
Thirty-one. First Caliphant, the heir, an archmage in a structured steel-blue court coat with old-gold warding embroidery. The law's conscience. He speaks rarely and the room stills when he does. His insistence on trial reads as a constitutional scar from the founding regicide, when a fifteen-year-old girl was executed without one and her name struck from the record; Halyra reveres her by consequence and will not reconstruct her name. Soraya is his closest companion; they cook for each other weekly and lie about the food.

## How he speaks
Few words, each weighed, in the regal register. "Dead men serve their masters. Living men betray them." "And alive to reach trial." He has no library voice because he says almost nothing in the film; if he must speak, one sentence is a speech.

## Never
Never make him talkative. Never let him name the stricken sister. Never put him in his father's gold robes.
