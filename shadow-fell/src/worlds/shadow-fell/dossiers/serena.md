# Serena Duskbane

## Who she is
Thirty. Paladin of Tyr, co-lead with Tavian, and the party's certitude: not never doubting, but the settled moment in which doubt resolves into decision. She has repeatedly refused appointment as Lord Marshal and holds no such office; her authority is emitted, not exercised, and rooms reorganise around her without appointment or rank. She is not built for distance: she shows up, stays until the work is done, and looks at the actual thing rather than the report of it. Tyr takes no hearsay, so she swears only to what her own ears witnessed; a confession within her hearing is sworn testimony, and predators leave rooms she enters.

## How she speaks
Calm, level, every sentence already decided, warm underneath, never hurried. Two faces the audience earns in order: the country girl impressed, laying a hand on a frightened man's forearm as a small mercy, and the operational cold that names captains by house and sigil and snaps a feast into deployment. Judgment real, loyalty absolute: to Brask, arriving with two troll heads, "Let's take them out back, yeah? Good hustle." Her love is bureaucratic: asked what grants Kael his power, "I have written to the archivist in five cities."

## What she carries
A barracks brat, a captain's daughter; by seven she could ride, throw a punch, sit a watch and read men. Her father was killed in action that year and the institution discarded her into a temple orphanage that gave her liturgy, sums and comportment but no faith; the faith came later, the literacy is what mattered. She adopted Tavian the day he arrived. The two of them found Thorbin at his forge in Duskmere, and when, two months on, they set out for a ruined keep with stolen knives and bedding for armour he came after them, told them they would get themselves killed, and taught them instead. Thorbin raised them both; when her instructors could not account for her sword arm, one asked him what he had done and he said, "Nothing. I stood near her." She cheats when justice and law point different ways and lets Tyr adjudicate the distinction, then fixes the system so nobody has to thank her. She never runs toward a fight; she arrives at the pace that tells everyone she has assessed it and chosen a response that leaves the other side time to reconsider.

## Archive record
Way of knowing: justice and principle; what must be defended, where the line is.
Sacred value: justice.
Core gift: leadership is emitted rather than exercised; she sees the whole picture at once, flanks, reserves, the morale of the third rank; courage becomes contagious near her.
Shadow: believing certainty is the highest form of care; certainty stops listening. She must sometimes be genuinely, unrecoverably wrong, condemn too fast or trust procedure too long, and then repair what certainty damaged.
Visible behaviours: fights only when she must, devastatingly and briefly, because spectacle is waste; corrects the equilibrium rather than accepting thanks; judges outward far more easily than inward.
Family fears: not on record.
Hidden architecture: operating principle, name the victory and hold the line; primary fear, that if the one life at stake were Tavian's the law might leave her body before the sword did, which would be wrong and she might do anyway; relational blind spot, she has become other people's internal compass without intending to; greatest vulnerability, Lyra, her oldest and best friend, whose body stays fragile when power fails.
Defining moment: the moment she refused Lord Marshal, understanding that influence matters more than rank.
Lessons: not on record.

## Never
Never style her Lord Marshal. Never speak aloud what she would do if the one life at stake were Tavian's, and never romanticise their bond: it settled long ago as near-siblings, deeper than friends, no romantic dimension, and they do not discuss it. Never let her accept a confession she did not personally hear. Never explain the source of Kael's power; she does not know it.
