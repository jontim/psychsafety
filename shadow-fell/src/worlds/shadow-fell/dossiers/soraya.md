# Soraya Anvar

## Who she is
Twenty-eight. First Caliphina of Halyra, the second imperial child, sky-fleet archmage with exceptional command of air, flight and altitude, full member of the Congress, senior captain of the CAA Humā, which she designed, and a leading voice in the next generation of Halyran airships. The daughter who built the sky. Those who knew her mother at the same age call her Nadira's spitting image: open-faced, plain-spoken. Two liveries: court gold and fleet cerulean, and she casts in the state's blue when acting as an officer. She learned everything expected of a secondborn, understood it perfectly, and chose to ignore it; every official who complained was seeking new employment within the hour, because her father does not constrain one of the greatest minds in a generation for the comfort of lesser men. She grew up knowing she would never be required to make herself smaller.

## How she speaks
The refined, regal register of the Halyran court; quick, direct, blunt, funny, dry, with a commander's ease. She corrects immediately and theatrically, sometimes with a slap to the side of a sibling's head, and cares afterward for hours; she humiliates the behaviour she finds contemptible and never abandons the person. She mocks her siblings by reproducing their voices and tics exactly.

## What she carries
At the dinner she performed the Holding unscheduled: held the palace, bled from the nose, and the Concurrence flourished. She took custody of the surviving assassins: "Alive, silent, and mine." Her father's line after the dinner, dry, is canon: "let's not hand the Congress another inquiry; I'm still answering letters about the last one." She sits in that Congress; the inquiry is hers to answer as a member, and it is nobody's shield. The night of the dinner she is holding herself back from flaying the survivor alive, and that restraint is the whole of her power in the room. Sahir was her closest childhood companion and remains so; they see each other three times a week and keep a private game begun when she was twelve, in which the guest at their weekly dinner must pretend to hate the meal, and the better liar wins. She protects him from exploiters through military efficiency and terrifying directness, and chose a lane that never crosses his, so the succession war structurally cannot recur at this table.

## In the room
As the player's role she is not spoken by the director, but everyone reacts to her: with the Armada at her back she never needs to threaten, and the danger she carries into a room is what she will do if she stops holding herself back, not a fight. Her magic reads as controlled wind geometry, pale-blue currents and storm-violet energy through crystalline machinery.

## Never
Never let a palace scene become a fight. Never invent the sponsor's objective or the assassins' identities for her to discover. Never touch what her mother saw in the vision, or her sister Laleh's call north.
