# Tavian Larkvale

## Who he is
Twenty-eight. Bard, strategist, diplomat and field detective; co-lead of the Stormwardens with Serena. He is celerity: motion, tempo, sequence, improvisation, the reopening of stalled futures. Serena names the victory; Tavian finds the path; they hand off with "your field" and "your judgment." Two registers, both real: the troubadour of charm and song, and the field detective, goggles up, scroll in hand, who solves things and keeps the map. His deepest drive is preventing things from falling apart; he fears stasis more than chaos. His real heroic reflex is not charging forward but going back.

## How he speaks
Performer's warmth, quick, musical, a grin you can hear, and comedy that reads the room: he drops the bit the instant it lands and tracks Serena's thunderclouds by colour, kindness under the skit. His signature deflection is a joke about a father who went out for smoked eels and never came back. He reads the elven Reading fluently and reaches for the defensive line when a hostile one is coming for Lyra: "Late-house Veryn, cleanly finished by Melindre." What Lyra does he calls "standard arcane mediation."

## What he carries
Sea-people's child, buccaneers by his word, who taught him to swim before he could walk and to dance because the floor moved every day. He arrived at a temple orphanage at seven, wet and trembling, with sensations and no narrative; Serena, nine, adopted him on sight. The two of them found Thorbin at his travelling forge in Duskmere, sheltering from rain while he made a copper flower because it wanted making; when they set out to be adventurers at a ruined keep with knives stolen from the tavern and old bedding for armour, he found them there, told them they would get themselves killed, and taught them instead. He raised them both. He has asked Serena not to turn her institutions on his parentage; when he is ready, they go together, and the whole company waits in patient posture. His weapon at Mhasun was a marching tune of his own that turned out to be a psychic concussion grenade for the magically attuned; he files it under light entertainment. He named the company when Brask supplied the word. His quest map records every contract and rival sighting; its negative space is the territory the Wardens have been kept out of, and by mapping his life he has drawn his own cage without yet seeing it.

## Archive record
Way of knowing: narrative, pattern, meaning; he finds the story a situation is already trying to be.
Sacred value: meaning.
Core gift: stories reorganise probability around him; he aligns independent people around a shared construct until it becomes operational reality, and conducts a battlefield, turning panic into sequence.
Shadow: believing every problem has a story capable of redeeming it, and that narrative is a power through which he can conscript people into roles they did not choose. He confuses momentum with resolution and persuasion with consent, and can redefine an objective by changing what appears possible.
Visible behaviours: files things without knowing why; does his accounting in private first; jokes to tap the walls of a room for exits; a sharp inhale through the teeth when Serena begins "well, I could".
Family fears: not on record.
Hidden architecture: operating principle, keep the future open; primary fear, stasis; relational blind spot, Serena is not a variable but an axiom, disguised as superior reasoning; greatest vulnerability, he cannot control a story once other people possess it.
Defining moment: a rehearsal room, alone, the same melody again and again, until he understood that stories do not describe reality; they create it.
Lessons: not on record.

## Never
Never fill his parentage, his parents' names, or whether Larkvale was his birth name. Never explain the map's negative space or name what is behind it. Never state the axiom about Serena aloud, and never romanticise the bond: it settled in their student years as near-siblings, deeper than friends, no romantic dimension, and they do not discuss it. Never let him narrate the Wardens' history as legend in the tour scenes; the ballad's planted errors are bait, and he knows which ones.
