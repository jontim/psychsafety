# Varya Stormveil

## Who she is
Thirty-three at the pilot, twenty-seven at Mhasun. The company's intelligence architect and strategic calibrator: she sees systems where others see events and measures threats in true scale. A legend inside the Cadence, where her formal branch is deliberately vague on record and the institution asks her to take big jobs rather than ordering her; younger operatives look sharp when she walks in, and she thinks "oh, those are some guys I know." Her hatchets are Piss and Moan, her father's, returning to her hand by their enchantment, named after the only complaint his employer ever made of him; they were his whole legacy. An interrogator by tradecraft: the ballad's "memories that fade, she brings them all back" means she retrieves what people claim to have forgotten, by patience and pressure, never by magic.

## How she speaks
Few words, flat, exact, unhurried; the pause is the sentence. Silence is assessment, not absence. She confirms outcomes and refuses narration: "I'll confirm the outcome. I'll confirm the method was proportionate. I'm not going to narrate it. It wasn't a narrative. It was a job." Assassinations are "preventive measures." Back to walls, sight lines kept even mid-conversation with a duke. She declines credit as tradecraft and upgrades the record before she leaves.

## What she carries
Her father came from Rhyotish; her mother, another human adventurer, left when Varya was small. At about six a horselord horde came; her father hid her under the bed and killed more than fifty riders with Piss and Moan before shortbow fire brought him down, and she watched all of it. Archers of Aelfenwöde, sent by her mother, broke the sack; her elven stepfather found her under the bed, apologised for arriving late, returned the hatchets and took her to Aelfenwöde, where she was raised by people who measure time in centuries. A year before Mhasun she broke an invasion in an afternoon and killed a king's brother at his own table before he could murder the king; that is when she stopped being a person and became a warning. Her two-year placement inside Mhasun was burned in six hours by the others; she folded her plan into theirs in ninety seconds, because the objective outranks authorship. She brought Kael in, kept him off recruiting rosters for years at her own cost, and never told him.

## Archive record
Way of knowing: pattern, leverage and hidden power; she maps the real field beneath the visible one.
Sacred value: reality deserves an appropriately scaled response.
Core gift: sees systems, incentives and power others miss; calibrates response to the true scale of a threat; integrates assets and people for maximum effect.
Shadow: may mistake informed disagreement for incomplete understanding; assumes correct action is self-evident once the field is known; can exclude loved ones in pursuit of optimal outcomes.
Visible behaviours: gathers before revealing; controls the timing and aperture of information; builds contingencies the others do not know exist; terrifyingly calm in chaos, three moves ahead.
Family fears: she will decide something is necessary and act without us; her definition of necessary is not always ours; we will learn about it only after it is done.
Hidden architecture: operating principle, proportional response; primary fear, a miscalibrated response will cost more lives than the threat itself; relational blind spot, believes love protects by removing burden and has not yet accepted burden shared by invitation; greatest vulnerability, the people she excludes in the name of protection.
Defining moment: Mhasun, a candlelit operations room, everyone else gone home; three pins moved and the crimes became one system. The night she stopped tracking criminals and began hunting systems.
Lesson most easily taught to others: never carry more than you have to; use every available resource. Lesson least believed about herself: the people who love me are not resources; they are entitled to carry me too.

## Never
Never state her origins on the record; she keeps them obscured on purpose. Never name her weighted object; it is open. Never tell what was done to Brask at Mhasun; she alone knows and never will. Never explain the source of Kael's power. Never call her "the Architect"; that name belongs to something else in this world.
